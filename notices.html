Notices & Announcements

Stay updated with the latest campus news

⚠ Urgent December 15, 2024

Water Supply Maintenance - Block B

Water supply in Academic Block B will be suspended on December 16, 2024 from 9am to 2pm due to pipeline maintenance work. Students are advised to plan accordingly. Alternative water stations will be set up near the main entrance.

Issued by: Administration Office
Academic May 11, 2026

End-Semester Examination Schedule Released

The end-semester examination timetable for May 2026 has been uploaded on the university portal. Students are required to confirm their room number details from their respective class groups. No changes will be accepted after this date.

Issued by: Registrar's Office
🎉 Event December 10, 2024

SAU Cultural Fest - "Harmony 2024"

The annual cultural festival Harmony 2024 will be held on December 20-22, 2024 at the Main Auditorium and outdoor grounds. Events include music, dance, theatre, food stalls, and art exhibitions. All students are encouraged to participate and register through their respective departments.

Issued by: Students' Union
Academic December 8, 2024

Library Closed on December 17 (Gazetted Holiday)

The Central Library will remain closed on December 17, 2024 due to a gazetted national holiday. Return deadlines falling on this date are automatically extended to December 18, 2024. No late fees will be charged for this extension.

Issued by: Library Administration
Hostel December 5, 2024

Mess Menu Revised - Winter Schedule

Starting December 16, the mess menu will shift to the winter schedule. Timings remain unchanged. A special dish has been added for Sunday dinners. Students with dietary restrictions should submit updated forms to the Hostel Office by December 14.

Issued by: Hostel Warden's Office
