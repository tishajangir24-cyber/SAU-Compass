Why SAU Compass?

South Asian University spans over 100 acres with multiple academic blocks, administrative offices, hostels, messes, and shared facilities. Finding the right place or checking if an office is open often meant hunting across notice boards and informal chats.

SAU Compass brings everything together — one clean, responsive website that works on your phone, tablet, or laptop.

  • ✓ New student orientation made easy
  • ✓ Real-time notice board in one place
  • ✓ Office timings always up to date
  • ✓ Works on any device, any browser
